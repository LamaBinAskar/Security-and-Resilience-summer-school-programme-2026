FUTURE09
Future adaptability

Retrofitting for incidents

Pre-planned CBRNE playbooks
Codified response protocols for chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear and cyber incidents, with clear roles, evacuation routes and isolation procedures.
Dual-use clinical & decontamination zones
Emergency and critical-care spaces designed to convert rapidly into isolation or wash-down areas, with integrated ventilation control and physical separation.
Segmented, fail-safe IT & comms
Network and CCTV architecture built in secure segments so a compromise or outage in one zone does not take down the whole system, with manual fallbacks for critical records.
Cordon-ready circulation & access design
Entrances, corridors and handover routes planned so sealed zones can be created quickly while preserving safe alternative paths for ED, ambulance arrivals and clinical handovers.
Inter-agency incident command interface
A dedicated physical or virtual hub for real-time coordination between hospital leadership, police, fire and public health during major incidents.
Continuous training & red-team exercises
Regular drills and simulation exercises to test incident plans, update layouts and refine procedures based on lessons learned.

Key learning

The hardest threat to defend is the one you can't see. An invisible agent, active for weeks, turns the hospital itself into the casualty — yet a hospital can't close. Secure the site, and you keep the whole service alive.
